Privacy Policy for Travel Documents

Last updated: 2026-05-13

Overview

Travel Documents ("the App", package com.myhome.traveldocs) helps you organize trip-related documents and reminders. This policy explains what data is processed and how.

Local Storage

The App stores your data locally on your device, including:

  • trips, document titles, dates, notes, and categories;
  • notification preferences and reminder schedules;
  • optional images or files you attach.

Advertising (AdMob)

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process device and usage data under Google's privacy terms. Where required, the App may show consent flows (e.g. UMP) for ads.

Google Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App may offer in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Purchase state is checked with Google Play to determine active entitlements.

Notifications

If enabled by you, the App uses local notifications for trip or document reminders. On Android 13+, notification permission is requested by the system.

Backup

Depending on Android system settings, app data may be included in backup/restore. Billing entitlement cache is treated separately and is not used as a trusted backup source of truth.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing (payments)
  • Google AdMob (ads)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent for ads, where applicable)

Data Deletion

You can remove your data by:

  • deleting trips and documents inside the App;
  • clearing app data in Android settings;
  • uninstalling the App.
